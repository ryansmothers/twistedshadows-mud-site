What is a text-based MUD?

If you have never played a multi-user dungeon — or only know graphical MMOs — this FAQ explains how text worlds work and how to start playing Twisted Shadows for free.

Frequently asked questions

What is a MUD or text-based multi-user dungeon?

A MUD (Multi-User Dungeon) is an online role-playing game you play through text. Instead of 3D graphics, the world is described in writing — rooms, creatures, items, and other players. You type commands like look, north, or attack goblin, and the game answers in prose.

Think of it as a living novel you share with other people in real time. Twisted Shadows is a free text-based MUD (sometimes people call these text MMOs) built in that tradition.

How is a text MUD different from a regular MMO?

Graphical MMOs show the world with art and animation. A text MUD paints the world with words and relies on your imagination. The trade-off is often depth: combat techniques, crafting, kingdoms, ships, religion, and politics can be richer because everything is driven by commands and descriptions rather than clickable hotbars.

If you enjoyed reading game manuals, tabletop sessions, or old BBS door games, MUDs tend to feel familiar quickly.

Is Twisted Shadows free to play?

Yes. Twisted Shadows is free. You only need a MUD client (or any telnet-capable program) and an internet connection. There is no purchase required to create a character and explore the world.

What software do I need?

Most players use a dedicated MUD client such as MUSHclient (Windows) or Mudlet (Windows and Mac). These make text easier to read and support aliases, triggers, and better logging than a raw terminal.

You can connect with a basic telnet client, but a MUD client is strongly recommended for comfort.

What is telnet, and how do I connect?

Telnet is a simple network protocol used by classic MUDs and many BBS-era games. In your client, create a new connection with:

  • Host: www.twistedshadows.com
  • Port: 1450

Connect, then follow the on-screen prompts to create or log into a character. More detail lives on the Play / Connect section of the home page.

Is this like an old BBS door game?

It shares the same spirit. BBS door games were text adventures and multiplayer games you launched through a bulletin board system. MUDs grew alongside that culture as always-online shared worlds.

Twisted Shadows keeps the typed-command, imagination-first feel of those classics while offering a large persistent multiplayer realm — closer to a continuous online campaign than a single-session door.

What do I actually do in the game?

You create a character, pick a class, and explore a continent-scale world of rooms — jungles, deserts, seas, dungeons, and more. You can fight monsters, craft gear, tame beasts, sail ships, join guilds and Orders, raise armies, and contest castles with other players.

The long story centers on reclaiming the Last Great Kingdom of Orion and hunting the scattered God Keys. See About the Game for a fuller feature list.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Twisted Shadows is multiplayer. Party up for dungeons, chat in-game, join guilds, form kingdoms, and fight alongside — or against — other players. Many newcomers learn fastest by asking questions in-game and on Discord.

Will I understand the commands as a beginner?

You only need a few commands at first: movement (north, south, east, west), look, inventory-related commands, and help. Start slow — look around, walk between rooms, and type help when you are stuck. MUD communities are used to welcoming new players.

Where can I get help if I am stuck?

Use the in-game help command, ask nearby players, and join the Twisted Shadows Discord for connection tips and newcomer questions. When you are ready, head to Play Now and connect.
